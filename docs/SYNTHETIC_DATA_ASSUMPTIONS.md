# Synthetic simulation assumptions

All values, relationships, fault progressions and alarms in this dataset are illustrative assumptions. They are not measurements or validated marine-engineering models; a marine engineer must review them before operational interpretation. No claim of predictive performance follows from this synthetic dataset.

- One UTC timestamp per minute for DG-01 / VESSEL-01 from 1–30 September 2026. The generator is stopped from 00:00–01:59 UTC daily. Running load combines a daily sinusoid and smaller deterministic variations; speed is approximately 750 rpm while running.
- Temperature and pressure relationships are deliberately simple: load raises exhaust, charge-air and jacket-water temperatures and affects lube pressure; seawater temperature contributes directly to cooling measurements. Noise is small independent Gaussian measurement noise.
- Five scenarios are in `data/ground_truth.json`: jacket-water outlet cooling degradation (12 Sep 06:00Z), lube pressure deterioration (17 Sep 00:00Z), cylinder 7 exhaust imbalance (20 Sep 12:00Z), frozen cooler differential-pressure sensor (8 Sep 03:00Z), and charge-air cooling degradation (19 Sep 00:00Z). Charge-air temperature is classified under cooling to align its tag, ground-truth scenario, and maintenance mapping. Fixed alarms are separately configured illustrative values, not tag valid ranges. Progression settings cross each machinery alarm at least once while running before the dataset ends; fixed-alarm evaluation must exclude stopped periods.
- Quality events include blank missing values, marked isolated spikes, bad-quality and uncertain records. Cooler differential pressure freezes at its onset value and is marked `STALE` after 60 minutes. Frozen behavior is a diagnostic clue, not proof of sensor failure.
- Candidate healthy training interval: 1–6 September inclusive, ending at 7 September 00:00Z. It precedes the earliest injected scenario. Exclude stopped intervals, non-GOOD records and values outside tag limits before fitting; a calendar interval alone does not prove health. Periodic data-quality events still occur in this window.
- The fixed seed defaults to 20260901. The generator uses only Python's standard library; `--seed` changes random measurement noise.

Limitations: simplified univariate-like relationships, no calibration drift model, no physical engine simulation, illustrative alarm thresholds, limited fault variety, and no real telemetry. Validate schema, ranges, alarms, baseline and fault behavior with domain experts and real vessel data before drawing engineering conclusions.
